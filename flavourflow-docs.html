FlavourFlow Documentation

FlavourFlow is an AI-powered cooking companion built for Pakistani households. It suggests recipes from the ingredients you already have, guides you hands-free through every cooking step, understands Urdu and voice, and connects home cooks in a social food community.

Using the App

  1. Create an account — sign up with email, or continue with Google, Facebook, or Apple. Verify your email with the 6-digit code.
  2. Set your preferences — choose your country, favourite cuisines, allergies, dislikes, and spice level. This powers personalized recommendations.
  3. Explore your home feed — personalized rows like For You, Trending, Cook It Again, and Jump Back In.
  4. Ask ChefBoo — tap the assistant, type or speak what you want to cook or the ingredients you have.
  5. Cook hands-free — open a recipe and tap Start Cooking for guided steps, timers, and voice control.
  6. Share & review — post to the community, follow other cooks, and rate recipes after you've cooked them.

ChefBoo AI Assistant

ChefBoo is an AI cooking companion powered by Google Gemini. It has two modes:

  • Chat mode — ask cooking questions, request recipes by name, cuisine, or ingredients. ChefBoo first searches the recipe database and only generates a brand-new recipe when nothing suitable exists.
  • Cooking mode (sous-chef) — while you cook, ChefBoo is scoped to your current dish: it explains steps, suggests substitutions, converts measurements, and sets timers.

ChefBoo adapts to your taste profile (learned from recipes you view, favourite, and cook), your dietary preferences, and the time of day. It responds in English, Urdu, or Roman Urdu based on your settings.

Food-safety note ChefBoo's suggestions are for guidance only. Always verify ingredients against your own allergies and follow safe food-handling practices — see the AI disclaimer in the Terms of Service.

What's in Your Fridge

Tell ChefBoo the ingredients you have — by typing, speaking, or picking from a list — and it returns recipes you can make right now. You can indicate whether you also have basic staples (oil, salt, spices) so suggestions stay realistic. All results respect your allergies and dislikes.

Recommendations

Your home screen is a personalized, Netflix-style set of rows generated by a learning recommendation engine that runs continuously in the background:

  • Meals for You — top personalized picks.
  • Trending — what's most cooked across the community right now.
  • Cook It Again — recipes you've completed before.
  • Jump Back In — dishes you started but didn't finish.
  • More Like This — recipes similar to what you last cooked.

New users get sensible cold-start suggestions (top-rated, preference-matched, and quick & easy recipes) until the engine learns their taste.

Cooking Mode

Tap Start Cooking on any recipe for a full hands-free experience:

  • Step-by-step instructions with heat, temperature, and linked-ingredient cues.
  • Smart timers that keep running in the background and alert you even when the screen is off.
  • Embedded instructional video where available.
  • Voice control and voice-to-voice Q&A with ChefBoo, so you never touch the screen with messy hands.
  • Serving scaling — adjust servings and every quantity recalculates.

Recipes & Reviews

Browse detailed recipes with ingredients, structured steps, nutrition, timing, and allergy warnings. You can create and publish your own recipes with a guided multi-step editor, and edit or delete them later.

Reviews: after you've cooked a recipe, you can leave a 1–5 star rating and a written review. Ratings feed back into your recommendations and update the recipe's average shown on cards and detail pages.

Community

A social feed for home cooks: post photos and text about what you're cooking, comment, like, follow other cooks, and share recipes. Every interaction can generate a notification for the people involved.

Notifications

FlavourFlow delivers real-time in-app and push notifications for follows, likes, comments, reviews, and cooking-timer alerts. The alerts screen groups them by time (Today, Yesterday, This Week), and an unread badge appears on the tab. You control which notifications you receive from Settings and your device.

Urdu & Voice

The interface supports English and Urdu, and recipes can be translated to Urdu or Roman Urdu on demand (measurements are preserved). Voice-to-text is available for search, pantry input, and talking to ChefBoo — making the app accessible to users who prefer speaking over typing.

AI & Algorithm

ChefBoo request flow

Each message is first classified by intent (greeting, recipe search, ingredient search, cooking help, substitution, recipe generation, and more). ChefBoo prefers real recipes from the database and only calls the AI to generate new ones when confidence is low. A safety filter blocks non-cooking or credential-seeking requests, and a per-user rate limit (30/hour, 100/day) prevents abuse.

Recommendation scoring

For each candidate recipe the engine combines several signals:

  • Behaviour score — weighted, time-decayed sum of your interactions (cooking a recipe counts far more than viewing it).
  • Content match — closeness to your spice level and dietary preferences.
  • Trending signal — community impressions, cook-starts, completion rate, and freshness.
  • Context boost — time-of-day and weekend awareness (e.g. breakfast in the morning).
  • Negative penalty — quick exits and abandoned cooks reduce a recipe's score.

The engine learns continuously: every rating, save, and completed cook improves future suggestions.

Security

  • All network traffic runs over HTTPS/TLS.
  • PostgreSQL Row Level Security restricts each table so users can only read and write their own data.
  • Passwords are hashed and managed by Supabase Auth; the app only ever holds a public publishable key.
  • All secrets (AI keys, service-role keys) live in server-side environment variables and are never shipped in the app or committed to source control.
  • Push tokens are stored in an owner-private table, isolated from public profile data.

Data Deletion

Last updated: 1 July 2026

You can delete your FlavourFlow data at any time using either option below.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open the FlavourFlow app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your profile, recipes, likes, preferences, and all other data associated with your account. The action cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Email us

Send a request to ffdevassets@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include the email address or social-login name linked to your account. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, email, avatar, bio)
  • Food preferences, allergies, and dislikes
  • Recipes you created, liked, or saved
  • Cooking-session history and analytics
  • Any social-login authentication credentials we hold

What we cannot delete

Aggregated, anonymised usage statistics that cannot be linked back to you are retained for service improvement and may not be removed.

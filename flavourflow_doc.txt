 Abstract FlavourFlow is an app that utilizes AI to assist with cooking and meal preparation. This app simplifies your daily mealtime preparation and shopping by suggesting recipes based on what you already have on hand, and connecting you with other users through a social network to create a community where you can share cooking experiences. You can search for recipes, generate a meal from what you currently have in your pantry; you can also manage your pantry using an organized inventory of everything in your pantry, including the nutritional value of what is on your shelves. Additionally, you will have access to an interactive digital assistant that will guide you through each step of the cooking process in real-time via a hands-free interface. FlavourFlow was designed by utilizing advanced technology and utilizes AI algorithms for personalized recommendations based on the ingredients in your pantry and personal preferences. Some of the features included in FlavourFlow are ingredient-based suggestions, a voice-to-text mode for cooking instructions, the ability to upload your own recipes and a social dashboard where users can post photos, comments, and share their cooking experiences with the FlavourFlow community. The goal of this project is to decrease the amount of stress related to the meal preparation process, stimulate creativity in meal preparation, and to help individuals become better organized and efficient at managing their meal preparation and cooking business. The use of FlavourFlow technology and ease of use allows countries across the globe to develop their own digital cooking culture; there are also certain groups of users (e.g. college students and people with busy schedules) who will benefit greatly from using FlavourFlow.

1. Chapter 1: Introduction to the Problem: 1.1  Introduction: The world is complicated. Cooking at home is one of the most complicated parts of living at home. People still ask, "What's for dinner?" every day. This makes it difficult to eat healthily. For people with dietary restrictions, they also have the difficulty of not being able to find meals made with their specific product allergy. FlavourFlow aims to solve this issue through technology. FlavourFlow leverages artificial intelligence to build an app tailored to each user's preferences, restrictions, and available ingredients. It can then offer up meal ideas that are both appealing and compatible with what users have at their disposal. With the inclusion of such features as "What’s in Your Fridge," community chat, and users sharing recipe ideas, FlavourFlow makes daily cooking easy, healthy, and fun for everyone! 1.2 Purpose: FlavourFlow aims to be a smart recipe system built around Pakistan's culture of food. Many cooking apps out there don't consider local food, the way each region grows their food and how families in Pakistan prepare their food, therefore FlavourFlow serves this area of need.  The primary goals of FlavourFlow are: • Provide an easier way for people to decide what to cook daily by offering intelligent recipe recommendations powered by AI. • Encourage users to eat healthier and discover a wider variety of meals, including diabetic-friendly options as well as low-carb and high-protein meals. • Prevent wasted food by providing recipes based on what users already have on hand. • Create opportunities for users to connect and interact with each other in a vibrant cooking community where they can post cooking tips, recipes and photos. • Introduce cutting-edge AI technology into the average person's kitchen with a user-friendly application.

1.3 Objectives: The core objectives of FlavourFlow are to deliver a comprehensive AI-powered culinary solution: • To develop an AI system that learns user preferences and suggests appropriate recipes. • To implement the "What’s in Your Fridge" feature with manual and AI-based ingredient input. • To provide a wide variety of recipe choices for every meal. • To offer clear, step-by-step instructions, embedded videos, and smart cooking timers. • To launch a "Community Chat" for users to interact and share their cooking experiences. • To display "Trending Recipes" based on real-time user activity. • To create an adaptive AI that continuously improves suggestions by learning from user feedback. • To integrate nutritional filters for needs like diabetic-friendly, low-carb, or high-protein meals. • To build a "User Dashboard" for users to upload their own recipes. • To provide Voice-to-Text dictation for search and pantry input. 1.4 Existing Solution Among the various digital cooking and meal-recommendation platforms available nowadays, Tasty, Yummly (Mealtime), Mealime, Plan to Eat, HelloFresh, and Nestlé Goodnes are some notable examples. The users of these platforms are provided with a variety of options like discovering recipes, following cooking videos, building grocery lists, or exploring curated meal plans. However, the use of these platforms is not widespread among the Pakistani households as the platforms are not able to cater to the practical, cultural, or dietary needs of that society. Platforms like Tasty provide short visual cooking tutorials but lack interactive guidance, step-based timers, or any intelligent personalization. Yummly Mealtime gets us limited AI-generated suggestions and grocery shopping automation but is heavily dependent on Western ingredients, U.S.-specific shopping integrations, and does not account for local cuisine, halal dietary rules, language accessibility, or Pakistani cooking styles. Plan to Eat and Mealime help with meal scheduling and basic planning but do not dynamically update their recommendations based on real user behavior or feedback.

 1.5 Proposed Solution FlavourFlow provides an AI-Powered Cooking Experience Designed Specifically for Families Living in Pakistan. Our solution consists of the following features: • Personalized Recipes Based on Your Preference: The AI learns your cooking habits via ratings, likes, previous cooking history, and profile information (such as dietary restrictions). The AI provides you with recipes tailored to your lifestyle and preferences • Smart Pantry: A What’s In Your Fridge? feature will help provide meal ideas using only what is on hand to eat or to prepare from ingredients already available in your home, eliminating food waste and relieving the daily burden of deciding what to make for dinner • Local Recipe Database: Recipes by US-based chefs who focus on traditional Indonesian or South Asian meals based on ingredients available locally and made with home-style cooking methods. • Hands-Free Cooking: A Start Cooking feature allows users to use the app to start preparing their meals hands-free. The feature provides timers, step-by-step guided instructions, instructional videos, and optional voice commands for instructions. • Urdu Language and Voice Accessibility: The app is designed to be accessible for older users, mothers, or those who do not speak English. • Social Cooking Community: Users can upload their own recipes and create connections to others through comments and likes to share recipes collectively. • Real-Time Trending Recipe Insights: Users will find out what recipes are trending in Pakistan and what recipes are being cooked the most across Pakistan, which will allow them to discover new meals being prepared by others. • AI learns and improves. Every time a user rates, saves, creates, or completes a recipe, the app improves its ability to provide better suggestions. This allows for an ongoing loop of machine learning. • Healthy Recipes for Diabetics, gluten-free, low-carb, high-protein, and halal-compliant diets, based on your personal needs, will be provided to you.

 2.2. Overall Description 2.2.1. Product Perspective FlavourFlow is architected as a cloud-native application, leveraging a modern microservices structure. The mobile and web clients (the "frontend") communicate with backend services via a secure REST API, ensuring scalability and maintainability. 2.2.2. Product Functions The primary functions of the FlavourFlow platform include: • Creating and managing user profiles. • Recommending recipes using an AI engine. • Suggesting meals using the Smart Pantry feature. • Guiding users through cooking with steps and videos. • Allowing users to share recipes and posts in a community. • Enabling users to search for and upload their own recipes. • Improving all recommendations based on user feedback. 2.2.3. User Characteristics • Tech-enthusiasts who enjoy trying different new and healthy foods out and sharing their thoughts on them, often referred to as 'The Millennial Explorer.' • Users focused on convenient, easy-to-make meals for families referred to as 'The Busy Parent,' who are often short of time due to other commitments.  • A category of users who are still learning how to cook and require step-by-step guidance, known as 'The Novice Cook.'  • Users whose diets are based on health requirements or advise from the doctor (such as with dieters or those living with diabetes) are considered 'The Dietary Planner.' 2.2.4. Constraints • The system must comply with all local and international data privacy laws (like GDPR) regarding sensitive health data. • The mobile application must support iOS 15+ and Android 8.0+. • A persistent internet connection is required for all AI and real-time community features.

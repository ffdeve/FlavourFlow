Privacy Policy

FlavourFlow · Last updated: 30 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FlavourFlow ("we", "us", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the FlavourFlow mobile application. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you sign up or sign in — including via Google, Facebook, or Apple — we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from the authentication provider.
  • Profile & preferences. Your display name, avatar, dietary preferences, allergies, disliked ingredients/cuisines, and similar settings you choose.
  • Recipe activity. Recipes you create, save, like, rate, or cook, including ingredients, steps, and photos you add.
  • Usage data. In-app interactions such as recipe views, searches, taps, impressions, and cooking-session progress, used to improve recommendations.
  • Device data. Basic technical data (device type, app version) and, if you enable them, push-notification tokens for cooking-timer alerts.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate sign-in.
  • To provide core features: saving recipes, scaling servings, cooking timers, and the ChefBoo cooking assistant.
  • To personalize recipe recommendations based on your preferences and activity.
  • To send notifications you have opted into (e.g. cooking-timer alerts).
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the app.

3. How We Store and Protect Data

Your data is stored using Supabase, which provides managed PostgreSQL and authentication infrastructure. Access is protected by row-level security and industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We retain your data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and storage.
  • Google, Facebook, and Apple — optional social sign-in.
  • Google Gemini (via our secure backend) — powers the ChefBoo assistant; your cooking questions and relevant recipe context are processed to generate responses.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Your Rights

  • Access, update, or correct your profile information at any time within the app.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see below).
  • Disable notifications from your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

FlavourFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact us at ffdevassets@gmail.com.
